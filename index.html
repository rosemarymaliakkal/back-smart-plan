Three full-body strength days, one or two easy cardio days, and one harder day for intervals or badminton. Every exercise keeps your lower back neutral and braced while your hips and legs do the work. Tap Log sets on any exercise to record your weights and reps, then check your daily report.

Missed a day? Do the next workout in line at your next session. Never squeeze two workouts into one day.

Leave a day between strength days

A cardio day, badminton or rest in between is fine. Two full-body days back to back is not.

Only 2 days this week?

Do the next two workouts in line (say A and B), then start next week with C. You still cover the whole body.

One hard day at a time

HIIT and badminton are both "hard" days. Don't put two hard days in a row, and keep the day before a strength day easy.

Log cardio, HIIT or badminton

    Your hard day

    Badminton

    Badminton counts as your HIIT for the day. You don't need both. Log it in the cardio form above.

    Warm up first

    Do the 10-minute warm-up, then 3‚Äì5 minutes of light footwork and shoulder circles before playing points.

    Bend the knees, not the back

    In deep lunges and when picking up the shuttle, drop through your hips and knees and keep your chest up.

    Go easy on big backbends

    Limit jump smashes and overhead shots where you arch far back, especially when your back feels stiff.

    Cool down

    Finish with the cool-down stretches. Wear proper court shoes with good grip.

    After every session ¬∑ 5 min

    Cool-down stretches

    Gentle holds only. Breathe slowly, never bounce. Add the hip flexor stretch from the warm-up.

    Rules

    Weeks 1‚Äì2Learn the moves

    Use light weights. Finish every set feeling you could still do 3 more reps.

    Weeks 3‚Äì6Build up

    When you hit the top of the rep range on all sets, add a little weight next time. Your log shows last time's numbers to beat.

    Week 7+Heavier lifts

    If your back has been calm, you can try a light trap-bar deadlift instead of the pull-through. Ideally get your form checked first.

    Every repBrace

    Tighten your stomach as if someone is about to poke it, ribs down, back neutral. Breathe behind the brace.

    Pain rule3‚Äì4 out of 10 is OK
