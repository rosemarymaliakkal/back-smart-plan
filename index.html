How this plan works
  • Always go A → B → C. Leave a day between strength days. Only 2 days this week? Do the next two in line.
  • Weeks 1–2: light weights, stop each set with 3 reps left. Weeks 3–6: beat the grey "previous" numbers a little each time.
  • Brace every rep: stomach tight, ribs down, back neutral.
  • Pain rule: 3–4/10 that settles within 24 hours is fine. Worse next morning? Go lighter.
  • Leave out: heavy deadlifts and back squats, bent-over barbell rows, sit-ups and crunches, standing heavy overhead press, back extensions, jumping exercises.
  • Badminton: warm up first, bend your knees not your back, go easy on big backbend smashes.
